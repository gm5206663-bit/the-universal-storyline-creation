SECTION 02

Core operating rule

Five failure modes killed a 90,000-word serial once. Every law in this system exists because one of them happened.

#Failure modeWhat it looked likeStructural cure
1No canon spineThe serial never asked what canon says happens in that forest. Canon says people hunt there for rings.Name at least five canon beats the serial touches, before drafting.
2Nothing contestedNo antagonist pressure. The protagonist was never wanted by anyone for anything.Every chapter gets a threat and a named face.
3No felt progressionRank and ring changes were asserted in panels but never earned on the page.Ring gates are chapter rhythm, not decoration.
4One register repeatedFifteen chapters in a single interior-meditation voice. Dialogue vanished from seven of them.Five registers, at least three per chapter, counted not eyeballed.
5Apparatus outgrew storyPanels, ledgers and codex entries became the product; the story became the appendix.Slim panel at top, no chapter footer, prose carries the weight.

[fan]Post-original-series material, fan consensus, or later-series additions.Weak - usable, never load-bearing

NEVER SKIP RECORDA decision that is not written down will regress. Every new decision, correction and canon receipt gets recorded in the same pass it is made. In this system that means filing it into intake/drop/ as a decision or correction, not just saying it in chat.

SECTION 04

Pre-draft gate

Six steps, in order, before any prose is written.

1. ReconstructREQUIRED
  • Rebuild the relevant canon scene by scene. Not from memory - from receipts. If a scene is missing, say it is missing.
2. Verify exact detailREQUIRED
  • Event order, location, timing, positions, clothing, objects, environment, dialogue style, motivations, character knowledge.
3. Build the ledgersREQUIRED
  • Timeline, location, prop, power and adaptation, knowledge - who knows what, and since when.
4. FirewallREQUIRED
  • Hide information from characters who have not earned it. Author knowledge is never character knowledge. Check every speaking character against the registry.
5. Resolve conflictsREQUIRED
  • Use the authority hierarchy. User words beat project docs beat kit laws. Record the resolution, not just the result.
6. Then blueprintPROCEED
  • Only now set beat order. Set the panel year range first. Then draft.
WHY PANEL RANGE COMES FIRSTSet the panel timeline endpoints before writing a single line of prose. Every date in the chapter derives from them. Seventeen separate date bugs were found that way in one rebuild.

The twelve locks

Set these before drafting chapter one. Write them into NO_MISTAKE_LIVE_RULES.md.

#LockQuestion it answers
1ERAWhich series, which years, which DC range
2PROTAGONISTWho they are - and explicitly what they are NOT
3CANON ENTRYThe specific canon beats this serial touches. Name at least five.
4SPINEWho wants what from the protagonist, and what they will do to get it
5POWER CEILINGThe strongest the OC becomes, and what they never get
6IDENTITYPublic name versus true name; what the reveal costs
7ABSOLUTESWhat the protagonist will never do, ever
8CANON IMMUNITYWhich canon characters' fates may not be rerouted
9MEASUREMENTHow rank and rings are shown on the page
10VOICEPOV, tense, register rules
11CADENCEChapters per pass, words per chapter, when audits run
12HANDOFFWhat a fresh agent must read first
LOCK 4 IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS MOSTWrite it as a full sentence with all three blanks filled: '______ wants ______ from my protagonist, and will ______ to get it.' If you cannot fill all three, there is no story yet - there is only a setting.

Blue Silver's cure was: Spirit Hall wants rings, and sends hunters into the forest to take them.

SECTION 06

The seven hard gates

Machine-checked by tools/verify.py in the Universal Kit. These are not negotiable, and "mostly passing" is a failure.

#GateWhy it exists
1Zero unreadable scriptCJK, kana, hangul. Not a blanket non-ASCII ban - em dashes and box-drawing are fine.
2Zero literal backslash-nThe two-character sequence leaking into prose from escaped strings. Use the real newline.
3Zero digits in proseExact figures belong in panels and ledgers. Prose says 'seven', the panel says '7'.
4At least three spoken dialogue linesDialogue silently disappears from introspective serials. It went missing from seven of fifteen chapters in one rebuild and was only caught by counting. Count it; do not eyeball it.
5Panels contiguousNo overlap, no gap. Insertion and reordering desynchronise panels, and the drift is invisible until two chapters claim the same year.
6No placeholder textNo shipped file contains template slots or lorem-style filler.
7Marker disciplineThe book-end marker never appears in prose; at most one end card, opening with END OF, last in the file.

GATE SCOPE IS NOT OPTIONALGate 6 must never be applied to templates, law files, or audit logs. A template with no slot tokens is not a template. A law file that cannot show the panel format cannot teach it. An audit log reports on placeholder counts - a line reading 'TODO/FIXME/TBD = 0' is the log doing its job, not a leftover. Gates 1 and 2 are the only ones that apply everywhere, because they are the ones that indicate a file is broken rather than a file being what it is.
THE SELF-REFERENTIAL TRAPA check must not match its own description. Five confirmed instances: a blanket non-ASCII gate flagging the em dashes in the prose law that explains the rule; a case-insensitive substring flagging 'placeholder' inside 'no placeholder text left'; gate 6 over templates flagging every slot token; gate 6 over an audit log flagging its own zero-count report; and documenting that last case inside the audit law re-tripping the same class. Scope the gate, do not reword the file.
SHIP THE NEGATIVE TEST WITH THE GATEShip the negative test with the gate. tools/selftest.py exists because verify.py once reported fifteen failures against a proven 33,100-word serial - every one of them a false positive. A gate nobody has tested is a guess.

SECTION 08

Knowledge firewalls

The single most violated law in collaborative fiction. Author knowledge is never automatically character knowledge.

StateMeansWhat it permits
KNOWNVerified knowledgeMay act on it and speak of it
KNOWN PARTLYPartial knowledgeMay act on the part held; must not extend it by assumption
SUSPICIONSuspects, unprovenMay worry, probe, deflect - may not state as fact
DISBELIEFActively rejects itNeeds direct proof to move; will rationalise anomalies first
UNKNOWNNo knowledge at allCannot reference it, hint at it, or intuit it
FALSE BELIEFBelieves something untrueActs on the false version; the truth must be earned on page
HIDDENKnown by author, withheldDeclared but not yet used in any registry
THE FOUR LEAK PATHSNever convert suspicion, intuition, rumour, or prophecy fragments into factual character knowledge without evidence. Never give a character information earlier than the earliest valid change recorded in their firewall. Predetermined destiny does not pre-install personal memories or trust. Instinctive power control after an awakening does not equal encyclopaedic lore.
HOW TO ADD A FIREWALLFive fields, no fewer: who, state, topic, earliest valid change, and rule. The earliest-valid-change field is the one that prevents regressions - without it, the next agent will helpfully let the character find out early because the plot needs it.

WHAT IT ISAn innate, non-conscious, existence-level personal law or instinct belonging to the user's OC. It adapts from the complete current foundation.

Compatible foundationThere must be something there to adapt. It cannot create power from absolute nothing.

What the validator rejects
  • An unknown kind, or a required field missing
  • A firewall state outside the seven defined states - adding a state is a law change and needs the user
  • A [canon] claim with no sources named
  • CJK, kana or hangul anywhere in the file (gate 1)
  • A literal backslash-n sequence (gate 2)
  • A reserved field such as authority_order or seven_gates
  • A duplicate of another contribution in the same file
  • A project id that already exists - file a correction instead
WHY THIS IS STRICTA state layer that accepts anything becomes a pile of notes, and then no agent can trust it. The rejection report tells the filing agent exactly what to fix, and the file moves to intake/rejected/ rather than being deleted, so nothing is lost.

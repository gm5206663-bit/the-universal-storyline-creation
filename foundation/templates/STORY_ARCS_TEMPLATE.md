**Foundation docset v6.0 · core · universal.**
What the serial is *for*, arc by arc. Plans are receipts, not prophecy: an arc
may change, but the change is logged.

## Rules

- Lock 4 is the one that matters most: '______ wants ______ from my
  protagonist, and will ______ to get it.' An arc that cannot fill all three
  blanks is a setting, not a story.
- One canon episode = one operational unit when planning coverage.
- Arc edits append a dated line — plans change; the receipt says when.

## Rules

- Confidence tags are the firewall for canon: LOW claims may not load-bear a
  beat. If two sources disagree, both are recorded with the conflict — the
  spine choice lives in `CANON_ACCESS.md`.
- The author's word can settle a conflict; it lands in `RULINGS_LOG.md`, and
  this file records the settlement, not a rewrite.
- One canon episode = one operational unit when mapping beats.

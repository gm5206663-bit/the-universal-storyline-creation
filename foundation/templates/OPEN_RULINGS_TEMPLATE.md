## Rules

- One row per author-only decision. No defaults on premise-defining rows —
  R2-style decisions (the region, the era, the protagonist seed) get
  **"no default offered"**.
- When a ruling is answered, it moves to `RULINGS_LOG.md` verbatim; the lane
  table row keeps a **ruled: {{what}}** note with the date.
- Collisions discovered between rulings open **new rows** — never a silent fix.
- The STATUS blockquote at the top is the gate's Stage 0 detector: it must say
  `DRAFTING IS LOCKED` or `DRAFTING IS UNLOCKED` / `drafting unlocked` in words.
- Closed Stage 0 keeps this file. It is the receipt that the stage happened.

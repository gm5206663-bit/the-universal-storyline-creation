**Foundation docset v6.0 · core · universal.**

is, what the ceiling is, and what never happens. The `system` pack's
`SYSTEM_SPEC.md` — if declared — is the implementation; this file is the law
above it.

## Rules

- A no-system serial bans System *vocabulary even as metaphor* — the Pokémon
  serial pays this as rail k10 and its style gate enforces the word list.
- Power inflation to force OC plot is banned by the method (competent canon
  characters stay competent): if the OC wins, it is earned in this file's terms.

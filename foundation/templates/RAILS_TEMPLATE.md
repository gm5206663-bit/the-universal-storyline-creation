# RAILS_TEMPLATE.md — this serial's laws

**Foundation docset v6.0 · core · universal.**
Rails are the serial's own k-numbered laws. The method's F-series governs every
serial; rails govern *this* one. Each rail = a law sentence + a check that can
fail. A rail with no check is a wish.

Precedents: `pokemon-adaptation/foundation/RAILS.md` (k01–k19, no-system
serial), Grey Wolf's style-gate rails (band, average, panels).

---

## Rules

- Numbering: k01, k02, … in force order. Gaps are allowed only if the gap is
  written down; never renumber — numbers are receipts.
- Style rails, if the serial has them, carry their measured numbers (band,
  sentence average, banned words) — measured, not argued.
- The no-system serial marks its ban as a rail with teeth: no window, menu,
  screen, gauge, percentage, level or stat — in prose *or any foundation file*.
- Rails never contradict the F-series; if they appear to, the author rules, and
  the ruling lands in `RULINGS_LOG.md`.
